Material Symbols Rounded, by Google

Source repository: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
Fixed source commit: 27e9ef1dbeedc13d682fece4a58e1eda4cb0961a
Documentation: https://developers.google.com/fonts/docs/material_symbols
License: Apache License 2.0 (see LICENSE.txt in this directory)

Variant: Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, optical size 24, grade 0.
Fill is 0 for regular symbols and 1 for symbols whose resource name ends with _filled.

Modifications: SVG paths have been converted to Android VectorDrawable XML.
The original path data is unchanged. The SVG viewBox origin is adapted with a
group translation, the fill color is supplied by the UI, and directional
symbols enable RTL auto-mirroring.
